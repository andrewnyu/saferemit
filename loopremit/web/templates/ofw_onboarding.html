  1. Create this device's signing key

    Your browser generates an ECDSA P-256 key with WebCrypto and keeps it in IndexedDB as non-extractable: it can sign, but nobody (not even Loop Remit's own code) can copy the private key out. Only the public key is sent to Loop Remit.

    Checking this browser…

  2. Link your PayPal wallet

    One approval saves your PayPal wallet with PayPal (Payment Method Tokens v3, merchant-initiated). Loop Remit then charges it in USD for each verified bill. You approve once; the plan you sign decides what may be charged.
