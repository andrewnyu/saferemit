How a peso moves

  1. OFW signs an open Payment Mandate (AP2 mandate.payment.open.1, SD-JWT) with a non-extractable WebCrypto key: allowed payees, monthly budget, caps per category, a step-up threshold, a 30-day expiry.
  2. Family uploads a bill. Claude reads it into a strict schema; deterministic checks compare it with the payee registry, the line items, seasonal history and earlier bills.
  3. The bill agent signs a closed mandate (mandate.payment.1) with its own key, revealing only the payee and cap it needs.
  4. The gateway verifies everything without any AI: signatures, digests, key binding, every constraint, the budget ledger, replay. It signs a receipt either way.
  5. PayPal settles: the OFW's vaulted wallet is charged in USD and the payee is paid in PHP via Payouts.
